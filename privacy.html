Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

1. Who uses the app

Hollow Moon Publisher is used only by the creator of Hollow Moon Collective, with the creator's own accounts. It does not collect data from any other person.

2. What data the app uses

Access tokens for the creator's YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts, basic account information (account name, follower and view counts) and the videos, titles and descriptions the creator chooses to publish.

3. How the data is used

Only to upload the creator's videos and to show the creator simple statistics about the creator's own accounts. Data is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

4. Storage

Tokens are kept as private settings available only to the creator. Uploaded video files are deleted after publication. The creator can revoke the app's access at any time in the settings of YouTube, Instagram or TikTok.
